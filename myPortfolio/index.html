About Me

Hi, I'm Christina!

My curiosity about technology began in 2020 during the pandemic. I was given the family computer to use for online school and Google Classroom, but there was one problem: no one in my family really knew how to navigate technology. My parents didn't grow up with computers, so I had to figure a lot of it out on my own.

From there, I started learning how to use Google Docs, Google Slides, and Google Drive so I could keep up with my assignments. Then I was gifted my own iPad and became interested in video editing. I spent hours watching YouTube tutorials and experimenting with different effects, captions, and sound effects, then proudly showing my creations to my mom. I loved taking an ordinary video and turning it into something completely different.

A few years of video editing eventually led me to coding. I realized I liked the same basic idea: having an idea and turning it into something you can actually use.

My first experiences with coding were a Roblox game development class and, later, an introductory Python class. I ended up leaving both after a few months because I realized I wanted more creative freedom. I didn't just want to follow along with an instructor; I wanted to write my own code, have it fail, figure out why, and try again.

That experience pushed me to explore the different areas of computer science, eventually leading me to cybersecurity.

I connected with cybersecurity in a particularly personal way. I've been my family's “tech person” for years, so I've seen firsthand how difficult it can be to recognize scams, security risks, and other red flags when you don't have much experience with technology.

Now, I'm continuing to learn, build, and explore how technology can be used to make the digital world a little easier to navigate.
